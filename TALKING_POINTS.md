## Objection Handling

### "What if Bob makes mistakes?"

**Response:**
> "Bob's code goes through the same PR process as any developer. The difference? Bob's mistakes are rare and consistent - easier to catch than random human errors. Plus, Bob learns from feedback and never makes the same mistake twice."

### "Can it replace our developers?"

**Response:**
> "Bob doesn't replace developers - it amplifies them. Your team focuses on architecture, business logic, and creative problem-solving. Bob handles the routine implementation, testing, and documentation. It's like having a tireless junior developer who never needs training."

### "How does it handle our specific tech stack?"

**Response:**
> "Bob analyzes your existing codebase and learns your patterns. Whether you're using React, Vue, Angular, Node, Python, or any other stack - Bob adapts to your conventions and follows them consistently."

### "What about security?"

**Response:**
> "Bob uses standard GitHub tokens with configurable permissions. You control what it can access. All code goes through PR review before merging. Bob can also be configured to follow security best practices and flag potential vulnerabilities."

### "Is this just ChatGPT with GitHub access?"

**Response:**
> "No. Bob is purpose-built for software development. It maintains context across your entire codebase, understands your architecture, follows your patterns, and integrates directly with your workflow. It's not a chatbot - it's a development team member."
